Metlink Synth

Connecting – buses – trains

Wellington's buses and trains, live and 60 seconds behind, played as music. Every busy bus route hums its own note and plucks one at each stop; trains whine, clack and buzz into stations. Late routes go sour, routes sharing a road bend each other's sound, and a vehicle turning around at the end of its route glitches. Zoom in to listen closer: the ring is how far you hear.
